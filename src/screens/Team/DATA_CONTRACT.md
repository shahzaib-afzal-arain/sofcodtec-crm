# Team implementation audit and contracts

The original Team screen has seven source-code fixtures, no persistence, static KPIs (18/16/9/3), static lead/won counts, repeated $82k and activity percentages, and an almost universally permissive matrix. None represents verified employee/performance data. Original names, emails and stable fixture identities remain immutable; local profile metadata/role/status may be managed without converting samples to real employees. KPI policy counts unique valid loaded records, including explicitly labelled samples; local versus sample counts are shown separately. Sales Representative means that exact role; Administrators means Admin or Super Admin; Active is explicit status.

Team owns only `crm-team-state` version 1: revision, members, policies, quotas, rules, history. Missing storage returns seven labelled samples in memory, without a write. Invalid versions/schema/duplicate identifiers or emails block all mutations and preserve raw storage. Saves are atomic, checked against the reviewed raw snapshot, and add local before/after history. No reset/delete recovery overwrites malformed state. Sample identities remain available in source.

Local operator selection is a simulation, not authentication. Mutations require a currently Active Admin/Super Admin. Self role/status changes, loss of last Active Super Admin or last Active administrator are blocked. Policies are configuration previews only; no global routes, authentication or APIs are altered. Invitation creation means Invited locally, never an email/token/password. Quotas measure locally marked won deal estimates, never ARR/recognized revenue.

Read-only sources: saved leads `crm-added-leads`; Pipeline, Activities, Follow-ups, Meetings and Opportunities version-1 namespaces. Original fixtures and explicit sample/demo intakes are excluded. Stable owner/member IDs or exact email references are accepted. Legacy owner strings require a unique, explicitly configured CRM owner-label binding; names are never guessed. Sample members have unavailable real CRM performance. Malformed sources/duplicate IDs are disclosed; affected metrics are unavailable. Dated events require timezone-qualified timestamps and valid calendar dates; reporting uses Asia/Karachi. Pipeline close dates remain authored date-only values; default currency is USD per Pipeline contract, other currencies stay separate. Activity tasks linked by follow-up activityId are deduplicated.

Assignment rules use only explicit saved lead fields (source/platform/country/industry/owner when present). Missing fields do not match. Enabled rules are evaluated by ascending priority; tied first priorities are conflicts and do not resolve; each lead has at most one proposed assignment. Targets must be Active real local sales-role members. No source assignments are written.
